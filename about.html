About Souq Heritage

Our Story

Souq Heritage is an online marketplace that connects Omani artisans with buyers in Oman and around the world. Many artisans still depend on physical souqs and word of mouth, so their reach is limited. Our website gives them a simple way to show and sell their work online.

Our Mission

To help Omani artisans reach more customers and to keep Omani heritage crafts alive for the next generation.

Our Vision

To become the first place people visit when they want to buy authentic, handmade Omani products.

Our Objectives

  • Give artisans a free online profile and shop
  • Let buyers browse by craft, region and price
  • Provide simple and secure ordering
  • Show product authenticity and artisan stories
  • Collect reviews to build trust
  • Support both local and international buyers
